About Redmond Flow Plumbing

We are the plumbers your neighbors call at 2 a.m. when a pipe bursts — and the ones they recommend at the school pickup line the next week. This page is about Redmond Flow Plumbing: who we are, how we work, and why being genuinely local changes the kind of service you get.

How We Started

Redmond Flow Plumbing began with a simple frustration: plumbing companies that treated Redmond like just another pin on a service map. Technicians dispatched from an hour away, call centers in other states, prices invented on the spot. We started this company to be the opposite — a shop rooted on 164th Ave NE, staffed by people who live here, serving streets we drive every day. We know which neighborhoods still have galvanized pipe, where the clay sewer laterals run, and how the winter freeze hits homes up on Education Hill. That knowledge is not in a manual; it comes from working these streets for years.

What We Believe

Three things guide every job we take. First, diagnose before you quote — we camera, test, and verify before we ever talk price, because guessing is how homeowners get sold work they do not need. Second, explain it plainly — you will always know what is wrong, what your options are, and what each option costs, in words that make sense. Third, treat the home like it is ours — drop cloths, shoe covers, and a cleanup at the end that leaves the place better than we found it. These are not slogans on a truck; they are the standards our technicians are measured against.

Why Local Actually Matters

When your water heater fails on a Sunday, a local plumber is twenty minutes away, not two hours. When a part is unusual, we know which Redmond supply houses stock it. When the city inspector has a question about your permit, we have worked with that office before. National chains cannot replicate any of that. And because our reputation lives and dies on this one community, every job has to be right — our next customer is quite literally your neighbor, and they will ask you how we did.

What Working With Us Feels Like

You call and a real person answers — day or night. We give you an arrival window and we keep it, texting when the technician is on the way with a name and photo so you know who is knocking. The technician diagnoses first, shows you what they found (camera footage included when it helps), and gives you flat, written pricing before any work starts. No hourly meter, no surprise line items. When the job is done, we walk you through what was fixed, what to watch for, and how to prevent a repeat. Then we clean up. That is the whole experience, every time.

Our Guarantees, in Plain Writing

Talk is cheap, so here is what we put in writing on every job. Flat pricing before work begins — the number we quote is the number you pay, even if the job takes longer than expected. A workmanship warranty on every repair and installation — if our work fails, we come back and make it right at no charge. Licensed, bonded, and insured, with permits pulled whenever the job requires them. Background-checked technicians — the person in your home has been vetted the way we would vet someone entering our own homes. And a cleanup standard: drop cloths under the work area, shoe covers at the door, and a final walkthrough where we show you the finished work and confirm you are satisfied before we leave.

We also believe in telling you when you do not need us. If your problem is a five-minute fix — a tripped disposal reset button, a toilet handle chain that slipped off — we will talk you through it on the phone for free rather than sell you a service call. Some companies see that as lost revenue. We see it as the reason you will call us for the big stuff, and tell your neighbors to do the same. That is how a local business grows: one honest interaction at a time, compounded over years. It is slower than advertising, and it works better.
